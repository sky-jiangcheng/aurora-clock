Aurora Clock - Options

Keyboard Shortcuts

Press a shortcut with Ctrl, Command, or Alt. Escape cancels.

Default Shortcut: Windows/Linux: Ctrl+Shift+O | Mac: Command+Shift+O

Chrome applies this to the real extension command. If the browser blocks the change, use chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

(Built-in feature, no need to set)

Press Escape key to quickly close the popup

You can also manage all shortcuts in Chrome's extension management page:

  1. Click Chrome menu (three dots in the upper right corner)
  2. Select "More tools" > "Extensions"
  3. In the upper right corner of the extensions page, click "Keyboard shortcuts"
  4. Find "Aurora Clock", you can view and modify all shortcuts

Tip: To avoid conflicts, it is recommended to use combinations including Ctrl/Command and Shift, such as Ctrl+Shift+[letter] or Command+Shift+[letter].
